SESSION POLICY
Assessment integrity rules
Leaving the assessment window or hiding its tab adds a warning, blurs the question, and pauses the timer until you resume. At five warnings, the session ends. Exiting full screen also pauses the timer. Copy/paste actions inside this page are blocked and counted as warnings.
TensorFlow.js face-landmark and COCO-SSD models load from a public CDN and run in this browser. The landmark model estimates eye direction relative to a short calibration; the object model flags visible phones. Three missing-face checks add a warning; sustained absence, repeated multiple faces, persistent phone detection, or a long gaze deviation can end a session. Eye direction is approximate and phone detection can miss small, covered, or out-of-frame devices. These signals can be wrong and are not proof of cheating. The browser cannot block operating-system screenshots or observe copying in other apps.
The microphone check measures volume only. Audio is not recorded or transcribed. Camera video and microphone levels are processed on this device. Use this as a learning prototype, not as a high-stakes proctoring system.
