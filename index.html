SESSION POLICY

Assessment integrity rules

This demo assessment uses five warnings. Leaving the tab, exiting fullscreen, or losing camera access adds a warning. At five warnings, your session ends and answers are submitted.

Where supported, your browser checks whether a face appears in the camera frame. This is an experimental, on-device browser feature: it can miss behavior and may not be available on your device. This demo does not claim to reliably determine whether someone is cheating.

Camera video is processed in your browser only and is not saved or sent to a server. Use this as a prototype, not as a high-stakes proctoring system.
